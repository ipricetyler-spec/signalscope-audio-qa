# Community code of conduct

SignalScope welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior, and protect private information shared during security or bug investigation. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub security advisory when public discussion would expose personal or sensitive information. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure, and avoid retaliation against reporters.
